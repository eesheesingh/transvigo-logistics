Account Deletion & Support

TransVigo · request account deletion or raise an issue

TransVigo is a workforce app; accounts are provisioned by your employer (the fleet operator). Use this form to request deletion of your account and associated personal data, or to report an issue. Requests are sent to our team at info@transvigo.in.

What account deletion does

  • Deactivates your login and removes your personal profile (name, email, phone).
  • Deletes or anonymises personal data we hold for your account.
  • Operational records your employer is legally required to retain (e.g. trip/expense history tied to their business) may be kept by the fleet operator per their policy.
  • We action verified requests within 30 days and email you a confirmation.

Submit a request

Prefer email? Write to info@transvigo.in with your name and the email tied to your account.
